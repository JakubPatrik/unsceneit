# Founder story 01

About 20 seconds, vertical, the founder on camera. The story is true, so say it in your own words and change anything you wouldn't naturally say.

## Script

| Time | Say | Words | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "Movie night with your parents. Then *that* scene." | 8 | You, close up |
| 3–7s | "It's in almost every movie, and I always look away." | 10 | You |
| 7–10s | "So I built something that blurs those scenes." | 8 | You, cutting to the product |
| 10–12s | "The story keeps playing." | 4 | Product |
| 12–15s | "You pick what you don't want to see." | 8 | Extension menu (`popup` segment) |
| 15–20s | "It's free, it's called unscene.it, and it works on YouTube in Chrome." | 12 | End card |

Times assume about 2.5 words per second. They're estimates, so cut the visuals to your actual take.

## Openers to test

Record two or three of these. Each fits in about 3 seconds:

- "Movie night with your parents. Then *that* scene."
- "That scene. Next to your dad."
- "I look away in almost every movie."
- "Movie night with your partner's parents. Then this."

## Shooting

- Use your phone, held vertical, in one casual take at home.
- Start talking immediately, with no logo or intro.
- Record clean audio. This video has a voice, unlike the six demos.

## Keep it accurate

- **Keep "YouTube in Chrome".** You're talking about movies, so people will assume Netflix. In comments: "YouTube only for now."
- **Don't list "cringe romantic scenes".** There's no filter for them. "Those scenes" is deliberate. Test how Sensual & revealing treats them before claiming it.
- **Keep any "clean eyes" framing about you,** not about people who watch those scenes.
- **Only use allowlisted footage.** No trailers or film clips. A real scene being blurred would be the best shot at 7–12s, but it needs footage you're allowed to post.

## After recording

Remotion can't take talking-head footage yet, because `SocialClip` only handles screen footage with text. It needs a new video type with word-by-word captions, product cuts and the end card.

Then send it through the pipeline as usual, and compare its "viewed vs swiped away" in YouTube Studio with the six demos.
