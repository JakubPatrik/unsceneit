## Keep it accurate

- **Not "any phobia".** Today's filters are spiders, blood and gore, sensual content and age limits. Custom filters don't exist yet, and lint blocks the phrase.
- **Not "every spider", in general.** It's true for this clip because it was corrected by hand, but the AI can miss one elsewhere. "The spiders get blurred" is accurate.
- **Don't say the AI caught them live.** This clip plays from the library, which was corrected by hand.
- **Don't say you can still hear the scene.** Hidden scenes are blurred and muted.
- **Keep "YouTube in Chrome"** so people don't assume Netflix.
