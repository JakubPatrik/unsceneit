# Story 04: Christian couples, and the music keeps playing

About 16 seconds, no face, captions on screen, with the same music track as story-01 to 03.

## Why this angle

On Nov 15, 2026, `story-03` ("Christian family movie night. Then that scene.") had 962 views. No other story had passed about 300. Its hook named the audience in the first words. This story keeps that, and keeps `social-05`'s point: the revealing shots blur while the music keeps playing. To avoid repeating story-03 (families at movie night), it speaks to Christian couples, which also matches social-05's partner.

That's one data point. A video's views also depend on the day it posted and on YouTube's distribution, so treat the angle as a test.

**Footage:** real shots from the founder's `social-05` take (IMG_0334), not the promo recordings: the "Gata Only" music video on the laptop, the extension's real blur, and the real dialog. These are the `gata-*` segments in `segments.json`. They show a third-party music video, which the allowlist normally excludes, so this is a founder exception with Content ID risk. The story's own music track is the only sound, and it **keeps playing through the blur** (`"muteWhileHidden": false`), like the extension with "Mute sound while hidden" off.

## Keep it accurate

- **Name the audience, not the content.** "Fit your faith", never "non-Christian", "sinful" or "immoral": describe what gets hidden, never moralize (STORY.md).
- **No song or artist is named in the captions,** even though the footage is Gata Only.
- **"The music keeps playing"** matches what the viewer hears: the story's own track. In the extension, that's the "Mute sound while hidden" switch, which none of these shots shows.
- **Say "blur", never "skip",** and don't single out dancing.
