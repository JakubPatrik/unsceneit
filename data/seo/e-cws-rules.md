## `seo/e-cws-rules.md` (Rules & Policy Guardrails Checklist)

To ensure full compliance before generating Phase 2 recommendations:

### CWS Limits & Parameters (2026 Guidelines)

* **Extension Name:** Max **75 characters** total. *Note: Front-load critical words in the first ~35 characters for visibility on mobile/desktop search cards.*
* **Summary:** Max **132 characters** total. Appears directly below the title in search cards.
* **Description:** Max **16,000 characters**.
* **Keyword Density Rule:** Never repeat any single keyword phrase more than **4–5 times** in the description to prevent "Keyword Stuffing" flags.
