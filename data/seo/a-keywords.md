## `seo/a-keywords.md`

### 1. Overview & Methodology

* **Scope & Target:** English-speaking markets primary (US, UK, CA, DE, AU, IN).
* **Constraints Checked:** Strictly aligned with `STORY.md`. Excluded all "NSFW", "18+", "live AI", "Netflix/Disney+", and custom filter claims.
* **Evaluation Framework:**
* *Evidence of Demand:* Search Volume / Autocomplete Presence / Trends Index.
* *Trend Direction:* Rising (⬆️), Stable (➡️), Declining (⬇️).
* *Alpha Relevance (1–5):* Score 5 = Exact fit for current YouTube alpha functionality.

### 3. Key Insights & Keyword Recommendations

* **Primary Winner:** `YouTube content filter` and `parental control` cover the broader audience, while `blur thumbnails` and `hide gore` capture intent for specific, acute pain points.
* **Front-Loading Strategy:** Front-load the title field within 35 printable characters (e.g., `unscene.it: YouTube Content Filter...`) because CWS UI truncates names beyond that point on desktop search cards.
