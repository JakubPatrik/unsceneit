## `seo/c-intent.md` (Search Intent & Audience Language)

### 1. Reddit & Forum Vocabulary Analysis

Analyzing discussions across r/Parenting, r/arachnophobia, r/emetophobia, r/youtube, and reviews for content blockers:

* **Phrases from Parents:**
* *"How to stop YouTube showing scary thumbnails to toddlers"*
* *"Block graphic violence and horror trailers on kids YouTube"*
* *"Is there an extension to blur thumbnail gore?"*
* *"Safe YouTube viewing without inappropriate clickbait"*


* **Phrases from Phobia / Sensitive Viewers:**
* *"Extension to hide spiders on YouTube thumbnails"*
* *"Emetophobia warning filter for video clips"*
* *"Blur jump scares and disturbing movie scenes"*
* *"Trigger warning extension for blood and gore"*


* **Phrases from Modest / Mindful Viewers:**
* *"Filter out suggestive or clickbait thumbnails"*
* *"Clean up my YouTube feed from graphic content"*



### 2. Audience Mapping Matrix

| Audience Segment | Primary Pain Point | High-Converting Description Phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parents & Families** | Unwanted exposure to violent/scary thumbnails & movie clips | *"Keep YouTube safe for family viewing without blocking whole channels."* |
| **Phobia / Trigger Avoidance** | Unexpected spiders, insects, blood, or emetophobia triggers | *"Automatically blur phobia triggers and jump scares before you see them."* |
| **Mindful / Sensitive Viewers** | Unwanted graphic content, gore, or unsettling scenes | *"Filter out disturbing content while letting the main video play."* |
