### 2. Competitive Openings (Where `unscene.it` Wins)

1. **Scene-Level Precision vs. Full Video Blocking:** Existing blockers either block the entire video/channel or disable whole features (like Unhook). `unscene.it` is the only lightweight tool that lets the story play on by targeting specific timestamp ranges and thumbnails.
2. **Zero Setup Overhead:** Tools like BlockTube require manual keyword lists or channel IDs. `unscene.it` offers immediate protection out of the box via pre-screened metadata.
3. **Phobia & Sensitivity Niche:** Dedicated phobia extensions on CWS are completely unmaintained or dead. Bringing high-quality YouTube thumbnail and scene filtering to the phobia community fills a massive void.
