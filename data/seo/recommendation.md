```markdown
# unscene.it: YouTube Content Filter & Blur Gore

unscene.it is a smart YouTube content filter that automatically blurs graphic thumbnails, hides gore, and skips disturbing video scenes. Protect your viewing experience from spiders, violence, and sensitive triggers without blocking entire videos or channels.

### 🛡️ How it works
unscene.it uses pre-screened video metadata to protect you from unwanted visual triggers before you see them. Pick your filters once, and keep watching smoothly while disturbing moments are automatically blurred or skipped.

### 🎯 What you can filter
* 🩸 **Gore & Graphic Content:** Hide blood, injury, and graphic violence in video scenes and thumbnails.
* 🕷️ **Phobia Triggers:** Automatically blur spiders, insects, and common phobia triggers.
* ⚠️ **Disturbing & Sensitive Scenes:** Skip intense jump scares and disturbing moments seamlessly.
* 🖼️ **Thumbnail Blurring:** Blur clickbait or graphic video thumbnails across your YouTube homepage and search results.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Who it’s for
* **Parents & Families:** Keep YouTube safe for kids by preventing accidental exposure to violent thumbnails or movie trailers.
* **Sensitive Viewers:** Enjoy YouTube without worrying about graphic trauma triggers, jump scares, or gore.
* **Phobia Support:** Browsing without fear of unexpected spider or insect thumbnails (arachnophobia-friendly).

### ⚡ Built for YouTube
Works directly on youtube.com and embedded YouTube video players. Fast, lightweight, and setup-free.

---
*Note: unscene.it operates using an offline pre-screened video index. It filters content frame-by-frame on supported YouTube videos.*

```
