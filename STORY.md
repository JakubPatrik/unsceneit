# SceneMod: product story

Oct 13, 2026 · @Jakub

How SceneMod works once it's in production, for writing marketing copy. It describes the product as intended at launch; where something isn't built yet, this says so.

## In one line

SceneMod is a browser extension that watches a few seconds ahead of you and quietly hides the scenes you don't want to see: gore, spiders, sexual content, or anything above your family's age limit. You don't have to stop watching.

## Direction for marketing

Lead with personal filtering: SceneMod hides only what each viewer chooses, at the strictness they choose. This is the core difference from every other filter and should anchor every campaign.

- **Positioning:** for people who want to keep watching but avoid specific kinds of scenes, SceneMod is the filter that fits *your* household, not a one-size-fits-all censor.
- **Lead message:** any combination of filters, each at its own sensitivity. One viewer blocks only spiders; another blocks gore and anything sensual at the strictest setting.
- **Supporting points, in this order:**
  1. It hides scenes *before* you see them (it screens about 5 seconds ahead).
  2. The story keeps playing: scenes are blurred or covered, not cut.
  3. Free for every title already in the shared library.
- **Where to point people at launch:** YouTube and video on the open web. Streaming services come later (see Claims to avoid).
- **Tone:** calm, practical and non-judgmental. Describe what gets hidden; never moralize about the content or the people who watch it. The audiences below have very different reasons, so speak to each one separately.
- **Call to action:** install free from the Chrome Web Store.

## Who it's for

- **Parents** who want to watch films with their kids without hovering over the remote.
- **People with phobias or trauma triggers**, such as spiders, extreme heights, or blood and gore.
- **Viewers who prefer modest content**: no nudity, sexualized scenes, revealing outfits or suggestive dancing, including the subtler kind common in music videos.
- **Couples and families** who want to watch together comfortably.

## How it works for the viewer

1. **Install** SceneMod from the Chrome Web Store. Edge and other browsers come later.
2. **Pick your filters** in the extension menu. They sync across the user's devices.
   - **Age limit:** Under 12, Under 15 or Under 18.
   - **Triggers:** Spiders & insects, Extreme heights, Blood & gore, and Sensual & revealing content.
   - **Sensitivity:** choose how strictly each filter flags scenes, so more sensitive viewers can catch more.
3. **Choose how flagged scenes are hidden:**
   - **Heavy blur**, or **Full cover**, a grey screen with nothing visible.
   - **Mute audio** while a scene is hidden. This is on by default.
4. **Press play** on a supported site. A short loading animation appears while SceneMod pre-screens the next few seconds, at the start and again after you jump to a new point. After that, playback is smooth.

When a flagged scene comes up, it's hidden and labelled ("Hidden: gore") while the movie keeps playing. When the scene ends, picture and sound come back on their own.

## What happens behind the scenes (keep it simple in copy)

- **Look-ahead screening.** While you watch a given moment, SceneMod is already checking the frames about 5 seconds ahead with AI, against every filter at once. Flagged scenes are hidden *before* you'd see them, not after.
- **A shared library of known titles.** Popular movies and videos are screened once and stored. Anyone watching a known title gets instant results at no extra cost to them.
- **Live AI for everything else.** For videos nobody has screened yet (indie films, user uploads, new releases), SceneMod analyses them live while you watch. The results are saved, so the next viewer gets them instantly.
- **It works where the video plays.** It detects video players on ordinary web pages, including embedded players such as YouTube. No video files are uploaded or downloaded; SceneMod analyses short frame samples as you watch.

## Why it's different

- **It's personal.** This is the main difference. One household can block gore while another only blocks spiders, and each filter can be set as strict as the viewer needs. Filters combine freely.
- **It hides scenes; it doesn't censor whole titles.** You can still watch the movie, minus the parts you chose to avoid.
- **It covers subtle content.** The Sensual & revealing filter goes beyond nudity to cover revealing outfits, sexualizing camera angles and suggestive dancing.
- **No jump cuts.** Scenes are blurred or covered rather than skipped, so the story keeps flowing and you don't lose your place.

## Pricing model

- **Free:** unlimited filtering on titles already in the shared library.
- **Pro (prepaid credits):** live AI screening for videos that aren't in the library yet. Every Pro screening is added to the shared library, so it helps every viewer who comes after.

Exact prices aren't set yet.

## Claims to avoid

- **Don't promise perfection.** The AI decides frame by frame and can miss a scene or over-flag a harmless one. Say "helps you avoid" or "automatically hides", never "guarantees" or "100%".
- **Don't claim Netflix, Disney+ or other DRM streaming services yet.** Their protected video can't be analysed by the current version. Support is planned but not built. Safe wording: "works with videos on the open web, including YouTube".
- **Don't say nothing leaves your device.** Short frame samples are analysed by SceneMod's servers. Safe wording: "no video files are uploaded".
- **Don't name the AI vendor** without checking with the founder first.
- **Account sign-up and credits** are part of the launch plan but still being built. Don't show screenshots of them yet.
- **Browser support:** Chrome at launch. Edge, Firefox and others are planned.
