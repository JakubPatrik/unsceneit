# unscene.it: product story

Oct 15, 2026 · @Jakub

How unscene.it works, for writing marketing copy. It describes the product as intended at launch and says plainly where the current build (v0.1) differs or something isn't built yet.

## In one line

unscene.it is a browser extension that watches a few seconds ahead of you and quietly hides the scenes you don't want to see: gore, spiders, sexual content, or anything above your family's age limit. You don't have to stop watching.

## Brand

- **Name:** unscene.it, always lowercase, including the ".it". It reads two ways: "unscene it" (remove the scene) and "unseen it" (you never saw it).
- **Domain:** **unscene.it** is registered and is the product's home (https://unscene.it). The extension links to it as its homepage. The site isn't built yet; a simple, minimal landing page is planned. Once it's live, use the domain in every call to action and on all printed or video material.
- **Tagline:** *The scenes you'll never see.*
- **Xenia:** the brand mascot and the face of the product. Use "unscene.it" for the product and "Xenia" for everything that happens while you watch (see Meet Xenia).

## Meet Xenia

Xenia is unscene.it's live AI avatar. She sits in the top-right corner of the video player, and she's the part of the product people actually see and talk about.

- **Look:** a small, flat, round face with two white pill-shaped eyes that blink. No mouth, no limbs: simple enough to read at a glance in the corner of a video.
- **She shows what she's doing:**
  - **Purple** while she's protecting the video, with an occasional sideways glance.
  - **Grey**, eyes resting, when you're watching without filters.
  - **Thinking:** a spinning ring around her and eyes that glance and hop, but only while she's actually analysing upcoming frames with AI. When she's just replaying scenes she's already checked, she looks normal.
  - The colour fades smoothly between states.
- **She talks in short speech bubbles**, for example:
  - "Checking this video against your filters…"
  - "Pre-screening the first 10 seconds…", with a progress bar
  - "Xenia is protecting this video"
  - "Protected · 3 scenes will be hidden"
  - "Watching without filters. Click me to turn them on."
- **She's the control.** Click Xenia at any point and the video pauses, the player blurs, and the filter dialog opens, so you can change filters or switch mode mid-video. Making a choice shrinks the dialog back into her and playback resumes.
- **She stays out of the way.** She's small, works in fullscreen and leaves with the video.

Copy guidance: Xenia is a guardian angel with a watchdog's alertness. She watches over you and keeps watch ahead, so you can relax. She's never a censor or a nanny: she protects you from what *you* chose to avoid and doesn't judge anything else. Xenia is always "she". Write her lines short and in the present tense ("Xenia is protecting this video"). Don't give her opinions about what's on screen.

She's the one doing the watching. "Xenia watches ahead so you don't have to" is accurate: no person looks at your videos, Xenia does.

## Direction for marketing

Lead with personal filtering: unscene.it hides only what each viewer chooses. This is the core difference from every other filter and should anchor every campaign. Xenia is how people remember it: use her in visuals, social posts and the store listing.

- **Positioning:** for people who want to keep watching but avoid specific kinds of scenes, unscene.it is the filter that fits *your* household, not a one-size-fits-all censor.
- **Lead message:** any combination of filters. One viewer blocks only spiders; another blocks gore, anything sensual and anything over 12.
- **Supporting points, in this order:**
  1. It hides scenes *before* you see them: Xenia screens about 10 seconds ahead.
  2. The story keeps playing: scenes are blurred or covered, not cut.
  3. Nothing plays until you've chosen: every video waits, blurred, until you pick how to watch it.
  4. Free for every title already in the shared library.
- **Where to point people at launch:** YouTube and video on the open web. Streaming services come later (see Claims to avoid).
- **Tone:** calm, practical and non-judgmental. Describe what gets hidden; never moralize about the content or the people who watch it. The audiences below have very different reasons, so speak to each one separately.
- **Call to action:** install free from the Chrome Web Store. Add "or visit unscene.it" once the landing page is live.

## Who it's for

- **Parents** who want to watch films with their kids without hovering over the remote.
- **People with phobias or trauma triggers**, such as spiders and insects, or blood and gore.
- **Viewers who prefer modest content**: no nudity, sexualized scenes, revealing outfits or suggestive dancing, including the subtler kind common in music videos.
- **Couples and families** who want to watch together comfortably.

## How it works for the viewer

1. **Install** unscene.it from the Chrome Web Store. Edge and other browsers come later.
2. **Pick your filters**, in the extension menu or right in the video dialog. They sync across the user's devices.
   - **Age limit:** No limit, Under 12, Under 15 or Under 18. A lower limit also hides everything rated above it.
   - **Triggers:** Spiders & insects, Blood & gore, and Sensual & revealing content.
   - **Your own filters (Pro):** if the built-ins aren't precise enough, for example a subtler version of an existing filter or a trigger nobody else needs, viewers can pay to create their own. Built-in filters have one fixed strictness; there are no sensitivity sliders.
3. **Choose how hidden scenes look:**
   - **Heavy blur** (default), or **Full cover**, a grey screen with nothing visible.
   - **Mute audio** while a scene is hidden. This is on by default.
4. **Open a video.** It doesn't play straight away: it waits, paused and blurred, while Xenia checks it against the shared library. On YouTube this happens from the first moment the page appears, so nothing slips through while the page loads.
5. **Choose how to watch:**
   - **In the library:** the dialog shows the title and how many scenes your filters will remove. Choose **Watch with filters** or **Watch without filters**.
   - **Not in the library:** choose **Turn on live AI filter** (Pro) or **Watch without filters**. You can also search the library in case the video is listed under another name.
   - The "with filters" options stay greyed out until at least one filter is picked. **Watch without filters** always works, and closing the dialog means the same thing, so a video is never stuck.
6. **Watch.** The dialog shrinks into Xenia and the video plays.
   - When a flagged scene comes up, it's hidden and labelled ("Hidden: gore") while the movie keeps playing. When the scene ends, picture and sound come back on their own. Nothing is skipped, so you never lose your place.
   - **With live AI**, Xenia first pre-screens the next 10 seconds, then keeps screening ahead while you watch. The same short pre-screen happens again if you jump to a part she hasn't checked yet.
   - **With a library title**, the results are already known, so playback starts straight away.
   - **Without filters**, nothing is analysed or changed. Xenia stays grey in the corner in case you change your mind.

**Thumbnails are blurred too.** By default, unscene.it blurs every video thumbnail and hover preview, on YouTube and anywhere YouTube images appear, before it can show, so a stray thumbnail can't give a scene away. Viewers can turn this off in the extension menu.

## What happens behind the scenes (keep it simple in copy)

- **Look-ahead screening.** While you watch a given moment, Xenia is already checking the frames about 10 seconds ahead with AI, against every filter at once. Flagged scenes are hidden *before* you'd see them, not after.
- **A shared library of known titles.** Popular movies and videos are screened once and stored. Anyone watching a known title gets instant results at no extra cost to them.
- **Live AI for everything else.** For videos nobody has screened yet (indie films, user uploads, new releases), Xenia analyses them live while you watch. The results are saved to the library, so the next viewer gets them instantly.
- **Changing filters is instant.** Live AI checks every built-in filter at once, so switching filters mid-video doesn't start the analysis over.
- **It works where the video plays.** It detects video players on ordinary web pages, including embedded players such as YouTube. No video files are uploaded or downloaded; unscene.it analyses short frame samples as you watch.

## Why it's different

- **It's personal.** This is the main difference. One household can block gore while another only blocks spiders, filters combine freely, and anyone who needs something more specific can create their own filter.
- **No jump cuts.** Scenes are blurred or covered rather than skipped, so the story keeps flowing and you don't lose your place.
- **You decide before anything plays.** No autoplay surprises: every video waits under a blur until you choose.
- **It hides scenes; it doesn't censor whole titles.** You can still watch the movie, minus the parts you chose to avoid.
- **It covers subtle content.** The Sensual & revealing filter goes beyond nudity to cover revealing outfits, sexualizing camera angles and suggestive dancing.
- **It has a face.** Xenia shows at a glance whether you're protected, and one click on her changes everything.

## Pricing model

- **Free:** unlimited filtering on titles already in the shared library.
- **Pro (prepaid credits):**
  - **Live AI screening** for videos that aren't in the library yet. Every Pro screening is added to the shared library, so it helps every viewer who comes after.
  - **Custom filters:** create your own filter when the built-ins aren't the right fit.

Exact prices aren't set yet.

## Claims to avoid

- **Don't promise perfection.** The AI decides frame by frame and can miss a scene or over-flag a harmless one. Say "helps you avoid" or "automatically hides", never "guarantees" or "100%".
- **Don't mention sensitivity sliders or strictness settings.** They don't exist and aren't planned. For more precise filtering, point to custom filters.
- **Don't claim Netflix, Disney+ or other DRM streaming services yet.** Their protected video can't be analysed by the current version. Support is planned but not built. Safe wording: "works with videos on the open web, including YouTube".
- **Don't say nothing leaves your device.** Short frame samples are analysed by unscene.it's servers. Safe wording: "no video files are uploaded".
- **Don't imply a human is watching.** No person reviews what you watch; Xenia, an AI, does. "Xenia watches ahead so you don't have to" is fine. Don't present her as a real person.
- **Don't name the AI vendor** without checking with the founder first.
- **Don't show screenshots of sign-up, credits or the live AI button** until they're finished.
- **Browser support:** Chrome at launch. Edge, Firefox and others are planned.
